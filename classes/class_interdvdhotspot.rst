InterDVDHotspot
===============

**Inherits:** :ref:`Control<class_Control>` **<** :ref:`CanvasItem<class_CanvasItem>` **<** :ref:`Node<class_Node>` **<** :ref:`Object<class_Object>`

A 720×480 highlight button that compiles to :ref:`InterDVDButton<class_InterDVDButton>`.

.. rst-class:: classref-introduction-group

Description
-----------

Place under :ref:`InterDVDTitle<class_InterDVDTitle>` or :ref:`InterDVDMenuPage<class_InterDVDMenuPage>`. The Control rect becomes the PCI highlight. :ref:`destination<class_InterDVDHotspot_property_destination>` points at a sibling title, chapter, or menu page; :ref:`InterDVDDisc.build_project()<class_InterDVDDisc_method_build_project>` resolves it to a title number.
