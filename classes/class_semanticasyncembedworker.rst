SemanticAsyncEmbedWorker
========================

**Inherits:** :ref:`RefCounted<class_RefCounted>` **<** :ref:`Object<class_Object>`

Background worker that refreshes asset embedding vectors.

.. rst-class:: classref-introduction-group

Description
-----------

Queues asynchronous embedding refresh jobs against :ref:`SemanticAssetIndex<class_SemanticAssetIndex>`. Poll jobs with :ref:`poll_refresh()<class_SemanticAsyncEmbedWorker_method_poll_refresh>` until they finish or are cancelled.
