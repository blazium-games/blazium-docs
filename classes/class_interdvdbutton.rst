InterDVDButton
==============

**Inherits:** :ref:`Resource<class_Resource>` **<** :ref:`RefCounted<class_RefCounted>` **<** :ref:`Object<class_Object>`

A PCI HLI highlight plus a domain-validated 8-byte DVD-Video command.

.. rst-class:: classref-introduction-group

Description
-----------

Menus and titles may have up to 36 buttons. Set :ref:`action<class_InterDVDButton_property_action>` to a hardware destination and :ref:`target<class_InterDVDButton_property_target>`, :ref:`title_n<class_InterDVDButton_property_title_n>`, or :ref:`stream<class_InterDVDButton_property_stream>` as needed. The inspector lists named titles and video cells from ``blazium/inter_dvd/project``. :ref:`resolve_command()<class_InterDVDButton_method_resolve_command>` encodes through :ref:`InterDVDInstruction<class_InterDVDInstruction>`: Jump Title is JumpTT from the VMGM and LinkPGCN from a title; Jump Chapter is LinkPTT or JumpVTS_PTT; Jump Menu ids 2-7 are JumpSS or CallSS. JumpSS from the title domain is illegal. There is no URL, file, or app launch — a video file on disc is a muxed VOB cell reached by JumpTT / LinkPTT.
