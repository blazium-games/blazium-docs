EditorExportPlatformWindowsInterDVD
===================================

**Inherits:** :ref:`EditorExportPlatform<class_EditorExportPlatform>` **<** :ref:`RefCounted<class_RefCounted>` **<** :ref:`Object<class_Object>`

Exports a burnable VIDEO_TS / AUDIO_TS folder, ZIP, or ISO.

.. rst-class:: classref-introduction-group

Description
-----------

Compiles an :ref:`InterDVDDisc<class_InterDVDDisc>` from the edited or main scene, or loads ``blazium/inter_dvd/project``. Disc identity and encode settings come from the disc node or resource, not this preset. Scene encode and ISO mastering spawn ``blazium-toolchain``. Dummy-VOB export works without the toolchain.
